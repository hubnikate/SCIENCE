2026 Offshore Sarangani Earthquake

M 7.8

Depth: 33 km

System: Cotabato Trench / Offshore Sarangani

A Mw 7.8 tectonic earthquake located offshore Sarangani, 32 km west of Maasim, Sarangani. PHIVOLCS associated the event with subduction along the Cotabato Trench.

Subduction Zone Trenches

Major offshore earthquake sources around the archipelago include subduction zones and active crustal faults; the June 2026 Sarangani event was associated with the Cotabato Trench.

Philippine Fault Zone (PFZ)

The Philippine Fault Zone is a major active fault system extending through Luzon, Visayas, and Mindanao and is an important source of crustal earthquakes.

Valley Fault System (VFS)

The Valley Fault System includes the East and West Valley Faults in the Greater Metro Manila area and is an important active fault system.
